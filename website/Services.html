Services

Technology should help you—not become another problem to solve. My job is to work with you to identify what you're trying to accomplish, explain your options in plain language, and help you choose a solution you can feel confident about. Whether that means repairing what you already have, upgrading it, or replacing it, I'll recommend the approach that makes the most sense for your needs—not simply the one that costs the most—so you can feel good about both the decision you made and the results you receive.

Minimum Service Fee

M&M Computer Help has a $50 minimum service fee for onsite appointments. This covers the time and travel involved in coming to your location, as well as situations where work cannot be completed because required information, equipment, access, or other circumstances outside of M&M Computer Help's control prevent the service from being completed.

Basic New Computer Setup

$50 Flat Fee

Basic setup is designed to get your new computer ready for normal use. This includes:

  • Unbox and physically connect the new computer and included peripherals
  • Complete the initial Windows setup
  • Create or sign in to the customer's Windows/Microsoft account
  • Verify Windows activation
  • Install currently available Windows security updates
  • Configure basic Windows settings
  • Verify built-in Windows security protections are enabled
  • Set up the customer's primary email account
  • Configure the customer's primary web browser
  • Perform basic printer setup
  • Verify Internet connectivity
  • Verify that the computer and connected peripherals are functioning properly

Advanced New Computer Setup

$75 per hour

Advanced setup is for customers who want their new computer configured specifically for the way they use it. Before making changes, we will discuss what you need and determine what configuration makes the most sense for you.

Advanced setup may include:

  • Optional Windows updates
  • Windows Insider Preview configuration
  • Manufacturer updates and utilities
  • Removal of unwanted manufacturer software and promotional applications
  • Microsoft Office or Microsoft 365 installation
  • Installation of other requested applications
  • Additional email accounts
  • OneDrive and other cloud-service configuration
  • Enhanced printer configuration
  • Additional Windows customization
  • Third-party antivirus and security products
  • Remote-support tools, when appropriate
  • Other customer-specific configuration

Moving to a New Computer

$90 per hour

Replacing an existing computer can involve much more than simply copying files from one computer to another. Before beginning, the existing computer will be evaluated to determine what information needs to be transferred and whether there are any issues that could affect the process.

Depending on what is found, this service may include transferring documents, pictures, browser information, email information, and other important files or settings to the new computer. The time required can vary considerably depending on the amount, condition, and organization of the information being transferred.
