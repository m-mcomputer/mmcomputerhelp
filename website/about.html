About M&M Computer Help

M&M Computer Help was founded on a simple belief: technology should help people—not leave them feeling overwhelmed, confused, or taken advantage of. The idea for this business came after I learned about someone who had been quoted an extremely expensive repair for a computer she relied on for school. Faced with a bill she couldn't afford, she was preparing to do without her computer altogether. Fortunately, the problem was resolved for a fraction of the quoted cost. That experience reinforced something I had believed throughout my career in information technology: many people don't need more technology—they need someone they can trust to explain their options honestly. They deserve clear answers, practical solutions, and recommendations based on what is best for them, not on selling the most expensive repair or replacement. Whether you need help setting up a new computer, securing your home network, troubleshooting a problem, choosing the right technology, or simply understanding your options, my goal is to provide honest advice, practical solutions, and clear explanations without unnecessary jargon or pressure. Technology should simplify your life. My job is to help make sure it does.
