Computer, Tablet & Mobile Device Purchasing Guidance

Buying technology shouldn't require becoming a technology expert first. My job is to help you understand what you actually need and make a purchase you're comfortable with.

There are countless computers, tablets, phones, monitors, printers, keyboards, mice, webcams, docking stations and other accessories available today. More expensive doesn't always mean better, and the newest equipment isn't always the right equipment for you.

I can help you make sense of the choices before you spend your money.

What I Can Help With

  • Computers — Desktops, laptops, 2-in-1 computers, Windows PCs and other computing options.
  • Tablets — iPads, Android tablets, Windows tablets and 2-in-1 devices.
  • Mobile phones — Help comparing iPhone and Android devices and determining which option makes sense for you.
  • Peripheral selection — Monitors, keyboards, mice, webcams, docking stations, external storage, speakers and other accessories.
  • Home office equipment — Help putting together a practical computer setup for work, school or personal use.
  • Upgrade or replace? — Help determine whether your existing equipment should be upgraded or replaced.
  • Compatibility — Help make sure the equipment and accessories you are considering will work together.
  • Understanding specifications — I'll explain processors, RAM, storage, displays and other specifications in plain English.

You Don't Have to Buy New

A new computer, tablet or phone isn't always necessary.

Depending on your needs, a quality refurbished or previously owned device may be a much better value than buying new. I can help you evaluate those options as well.

The goal isn't to sell you the newest or most expensive equipment. The goal is to help you get equipment that is appropriate for what you actually need to do.

Computers, Tablets and Phones

The right device depends on what you want to accomplish with it. Someone who primarily browses the web, checks email and watches videos may have very different needs from someone who works with large documents, photographs, business applications or other demanding software.

The same applies to tablets and mobile phones. If you already use several devices from the same manufacturer, staying within that ecosystem may provide advantages that aren't immediately obvious.

For example, someone who already uses an iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods may find an iPad a natural addition to their existing setup. Someone who primarily uses Windows and Microsoft services may have different priorities when choosing a tablet or phone.

I can help you look beyond the specifications and consider how the device will actually fit into the technology you already use.

Three Ways I Can Help

Tell Me What You Need

Tell me what you use your computer, tablet or phone for, what you would like to be able to do, and what you are comfortable spending. I'll help identify the type of equipment that makes sense for you.

Show Me What You're Considering

Found a computer, tablet, phone or accessory you think might be right for you? Send me the specifications or show me what you're looking at. I'll help you determine whether it is a good fit and whether there may be better alternatives.

Want Someone With You While You Shop?

If you would rather have someone with you while shopping, I can accompany you and help evaluate the equipment you're considering.

Whether you're shopping at a local store, looking at refurbished equipment, or comparing several options, I can help you make sense of what you're seeing before you spend your money.

My Goal Is Simple

I'm not trying to sell you a computer. I'm trying to help you buy the right technology.

A $1,500 computer isn't necessarily a better choice for you than a $700 computer. It depends on what you need it to do.

My recommendations are based on your needs, your budget and the equipment that makes the most sense for your situation.

Mobile Device Purchasing — Yes. Mobile Device Repair — No.

I am happy to help you choose a phone or tablet, but I do not repair mobile devices.

Modern phones and tablets often require specialized tools, parts, training and repair techniques. I don't have the specialized equipment or training required for this type of repair, and I don't want to take apart your device simply to discover that I cannot properly repair it.

This includes screen, battery, charging-port, button, internal component and board-level repairs.

If your phone or tablet needs repair, I can help you determine whether repairing it makes financial sense or whether replacing it would be the better option.

You're in Control of the Purchase

I provide recommendations and guidance, but you remain in control of the purchase. You purchase the equipment directly from the retailer or seller.

I do not need your credit card or banking information, and I don't purchase equipment using your financial information.

Once you've made your purchase, I can also help with the next step: setting up your new computer, transferring your data, installing applications and making sure everything is ready to use.

From Choosing to Using

Need help deciding what to buy? I can help you compare your options before you purchase.

Once you've made your decision, I can also help with setup, configuration, data transfer, applications and getting everything ready for you to use.

Honest advice before you buy. Practical help after you bring it home.
